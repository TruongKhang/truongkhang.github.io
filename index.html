Khang Truong Giang

3D Vision Engineer at 42dot, Hyundai Motor Group · Ph.D., KAIST

Email Scholar GitHub CV

I am a 3D vision researcher working on 3D reconstruction and neural rendering from 2D images. I currently build multi-camera perception for autonomous driving at 42dot, the global software center of Hyundai Motor Group.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from KAIST in 2024, advised by Prof. Sungho Jo and Prof. Soohwan Song, where my research spanned multi-view stereo, feature matching, and visual localization. My work has appeared at CVPR, ICLR, AAAI, IJCAI, and IEEE TIP.

Research interests

  • 3D reconstruction
  • Multi-view stereo
  • Feature matching
  • Visual localization
  • 3D Gaussian splatting
  • Autonomous driving perception
